Known gaps and unclear areas

This model is a simplified teaching model. Positions, sizes and shapes are approximate, and the areas below are the ones most likely to be off, thin or missing. Back to the model

Body surface and proportions

  • The male and female body surface meshes (uploads/…BaseMesh.glb) are not in the repository. Without them the generic built skin is shown for both sexes, with no subcutaneous fat layer.
  • Organ sizes run about 60 to 80% of textbook measurements so neighbouring structures fit without overlap.
  • Fine surface textures (gastric pits, papillae, skin micro-rugae) are left out.

Vessels and lymphatics

  • The femoral artery and vein run from the aortic end through the pelvis and down the limb as one tube. The iliac and popliteal vessels are drawn over that course, so the pelvic part lies deeper than in a real body, and the course through the knee passes through bone.
  • Only the larger named vessels are modelled. Smaller branches, the dorsal venous networks of the hand and foot, and most of the foot and hand vessels are missing or schematic.
  • The lymphatic system is sparse: lymph nodes, the thymus, the spleen, the thoracic duct and a few vessels only.
  • The saphenous veins follow the surface of the fascial sleeves, so their depth is approximate.

Muscles and fascia

  • Fascial sleeves are see-through shells fitted to the outer surface of the muscles they wrap. They do not show septa, compartments or thickness.
  • Rotatores, intertransversarii and other small deep back muscles are not modelled. Multifidus is a single strip per side.
  • The levator ani (pubococcygeus and iliococcygeus) is drawn as two flat sheets, not a full funnel, and the rest of the pelvic floor and perineum is simplified.
  • Psoas major, iliacus and quadratus lumborum are placed by landmarks and may sit slightly off the bone surfaces they use.

Joints and ligaments

  • The knee is built from two spheres, a cartilage disc and a tibial plateau. The cruciate ligaments and menisci are placed inside that simplified joint, so the cruciates are hidden by bone. Select one and lower the Surrounding anatomy slider to see it.
  • Spinal ligaments (anterior and posterior longitudinal, ligamentum flavum) are thin continuous strips, not one band per vertebral level.
  • The glenoid labrum, glenohumeral ligaments and iliofemoral ligament are approximate bands on a simplified shoulder and hip.
  • Most other joint capsules are not modelled.

Female reproductive system and breast

  • Female structures are schematic. The uterus, ovaries and tubes are simple forms, and the 16 breast lobes, acini and ducts are regular patterns, not a dissection.
  • The menstrual cycle slider changes endometrial thickness, the follicle and the corpus luteum on the right ovary only.

Layers and explode mode

  • Structures named in the reference list sit in the layer it gives. Structures it does not name are placed by depth, and some of those placements are judgement calls.
  • Explode mode sorts each structure into a region by its centre point, so very long structures (the aorta, long nerves) can appear cut off or missing in a regional view.
  • The Lymphatics column in explode mode holds only the few lymphatic structures above.

Other

  • The cross-section view is flat-coloured and approximate, and is hidden in explode mode.
  • This model is for study and illustration. It is not for clinical or surgical planning.
